# Digital Products Revenue Engine

## Overview
A systematic approach to generating passive income through digital products. Research-driven strategy for creating, marketing, and selling templates, prompts, courses, and SaaS tools.

## Problem
Service-based income requires constant effort:
- Trading time for money limits scalability
- Client work has no residual value
- No passive income streams
- Need revenue source that doesn't require live calls

## Solution
A tiered product portfolio designed for quick wins + long-term growth:

### Phase 1: Quick Wins (Week 1-2)
| Product | Price | Effort | Revenue Potential |
|---------|-------|--------|-------------------|
| AI Prompt Pack | $29 | Low | $100-500/mo |
| Notion Template | $19 | Low | $100-300/mo |
| Quick Start Guide | $17 | Low | $50-200/mo |

## Tech Stack
- **Payments:** Stripe or Lemon Squeezy (5% fees)
- **Delivery:** Gumroad or self-hosted
- **Email:** Resend for automation
- **Landing Pages:** Next.js (reusing MC stack)

## Sales Funnel
```
Social Media Post
       ↓
Free Value (Short/Guide)
       ↓
Landing Page → Email Capture
       ↓
3-5 Email Sequence
       ↓
Product Offer ($27-97)
       ↓
Upsell ($197-697)
       ↓
Recurring ($29-99/mo)
```
